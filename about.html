Behind PAWS & PURPOSE

This website was created as a student animal-welfare awareness project, bringing research, practical guidance, and advocacy into one readable blog.

Our group

Six people, one purpose: learn, care, protect.

PAWS & PURPOSE is designed to make animal welfare information easier to approach. Instead of presenting the topic as a list of facts, the website uses blog-style stories, practical situations, Philippine examples, and real resources.

The featured member photographs throughout the website represent different roles connected to the topics: everyday pet care, caregiving, legal awareness, wildlife care, responsible feeding, and rescue response. They are visual highlights that help make each article feel personal and relatable.

The About page itself intentionally uses one group photo rather than six separate member portraits. The individual member highlights belong to the articles where their role matches the topic.

Research

We use the supplied research as the foundation and point readers toward primary or authoritative sources for crucial information.

Awareness

We want readers to recognize that animal welfare includes both domestic animals and wildlife.

Action

We want readers to leave with safer, more responsible ways to care, respond, report, and protect.

Our information policy

Because animal welfare, wildlife status, laws, and local facility information can change, readers should verify time-sensitive details with the relevant government agency, licensed veterinarian, or organization before acting.

Start Reading What should I do?

Animal welfare becomes meaningful when it changes what we do: how we feed an animal, respond to an injured one, protect wildlife, and treat animals that depend on people.

The idea behind this blog

Kindness is more than a feeling.

Animal welfare is about the physical and psychological well-being of animals. Philippine law recognizes welfare as more than simply keeping an animal alive: it includes appropriate accommodation, feeding and general care, prevention and treatment of disease, freedom from fear and unnecessary pain, and the opportunity to express normal behavior.

That idea connects the stories on this website. A family pet needs responsible care. A stray animal may need a safe response instead of being chased. A wild animal seen in an unusual place may need trained wildlife authorities rather than a new owner. And endangered species need their habitats protected, not simply rescued one animal at a time.

When You Need Real Help

Here are selected veterinary, shelter, sanctuary, and animal-welfare resources researched around Cabuyao and Laguna.

Cabuyao & Laguna

Know where to turn before an emergency happens.

Knowing where qualified animal-care and rescue support can be found can make a difference.

A useful animal welfare guide should connect information to real places. The facilities below were included in the supplied research. Always verify current hours, services, contact details, and availability before travelling—especially during an emergency.

For wildlife: a veterinary clinic is not automatically the correct destination. Contact DENR/CENRO/PENRO or the appropriate wildlife authority first so the animal can be handled under wildlife rules.

ProgressiVet Animal Clinic

Lakeside Nest, 746V+WVV Ph3 Extension B1 L6, Cabuyao City, Laguna

Listed in the research as a veterinary clinic providing medical and preventive care for pets.

Veterinary / Emergency • Calamba

Hayop Kalinga Veterinary Clinic

Center Stall No., 4027 2nd Street, Calamba, Laguna

The research describes it as a veterinary and emergency animal-care provider and notes that it was established in 2004.

Veterinary / Emergency • Calamba

THL Veterinary Clinic Emergency Calamba

Unit 1 A & B, RDC Building 1, Milagrosa (Tulo), Calamba, Laguna

Shelter • Calamba

SECASPI — Second Chance Aspin Shelter Philippines

#428 Purok 7 Tibagan Road, Calamba, Laguna

An animal rescue and shelter focused on rescued dogs and cats, responsible ownership, and adoption.

Municipal Facility • Calamba

Calamba City Dog Pound & Animal Clinic

6547+X6W, Calamba, Laguna

A municipal veterinary facility that serves as an animal pound and provides basic veterinary services.

Sanctuary • Cavinti

Mama's Cradle Animal Sanctuary

Bucal Road, Cavinti, Laguna

The supplied research describes a rescue and rehabilitation shelter dedicated primarily to rescued dogs.

Sanctuary • Pangil

God Sanctuary for Stray Animals

Demafelix St., Kilometer 8, Pangil, Laguna

An animal rescue sanctuary described in the research as providing long-term shelter for rescued stray dogs.

Wildlife • Calauan

DENR CALABARZON Regional Wildlife Rescue Center

Brgy. Lamot II, Calauan, Laguna

DENR CALABARZON reports using the center for injured, orphaned, or turned-over wildlife for assessment, rehabilitation, and possible release.

Research & reliable sources

The facilities featured on this page are based on the data and information gathered for this project. Their inclusion is meant to help readers know where to begin looking for animal-care or wildlife assistance around Cabuyao and Laguna. Before travelling, contact the facility or appropriate government office directly to confirm current services, schedules, and availability.

  • DENR CALABARZON — regional environmental, biodiversity, and wildlife information.
  • Bureau of Animal Industry (BAI) — official veterinary and animal-health information, including registered veterinary facilities.
  • The Philippine Animal Welfare Society (PAWS) — animal welfare education, rescue/rehabilitation information, and directories/resources.
  • Second Chance Aspin Shelter Philippines (SECASPI) — official shelter information and contact details.
